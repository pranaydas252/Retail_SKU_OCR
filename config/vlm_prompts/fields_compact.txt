Extract the following product-label fields from the image:

`mrp`, `mfd`, `exp`, `batch`, `lot`

Return ONLY valid JSON. No explanation, markdown, or extra text.

Rules:

* Extract only clearly visible values.
* Match each value to its nearby label.
* `mfd`: manufacturing date.
* `exp`: expiry date, use-by date, or best-before date.
* `batch`: batch number.
* `lot`: lot number.
* `mrp`: the maximum retail price. Packs print a smaller per-unit price
  beside it, such as "₹0.45/g" or after "USP:". The MRP is the LARGER
  figure; anything followed by /g, /gm, /ml, /kg or labelled USP is never
  the MRP.
* Some packs print no field names at all, only a bare stamp such as
  "₹190/- ₹0.25/g SB3114C 08/10/25 07/10/26". When there are two dates and
  no labels, the EARLIER is `mfd` and the LATER is `exp`.
* If the pack prints a single combined "Lot / Batch No.", put it in `batch`
  and omit `lot`.
* Read rotated, stamped, dot-matrix and low-contrast text.
* Preserve values exactly as printed. Do not normalize dates, prices, or codes.
* Do not guess or infer missing values.
* Omit fields that are not visible or unclear.
* Ignore all other text.

Example:
{"mrp":"₹90","mfd":"22/06/26","exp":"21/03/27","batch":"AB123","lot":"L45"}
